Why restoration is fascinating

Furniture restoration blends patience, craftsmanship, and practical problem-solving. A scratched chair or faded table can become useful again with the right repair and finish.

Restoration process

  1. Find a piece worth saving
    • Solid wood chairs
    • Dressers with strong frames
    • Tables with restorable surfaces
  2. Clean and inspect the piece
  3. Repair damage and reinforce joints
  4. Sand, stain, or repaint
  5. Style and resell or reuse

Featured Image

A simple chair can look completely different after a little work.

Why it matters

Restoring furniture helps keep usable materials out of landfills. It also gives people affordable access to interesting home items that feel more personal than factory-made furniture.
